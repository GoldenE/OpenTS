The previous `Cell(int)` audit was wrong: aircraft, building, and vehicle legacy INI readers actively used it. Each now explicitly preserves the old 128-column decoding. The previous plan also incorrectly proposed shortening positional overlay streams and initialization of retained cells, and treated `Validate`'s `#if NEVER` code as an active scan. These corrections are part of completing the safe scope, not permission to change map formats. Both map constants stay 512, and `Encroach_Fog` is unchanged.

Live 256×256 loading exposed an iterator that computed an out-of-table next pointer after its last valid cell; it now terminates explicitly at the physical table boundary. Selecting a previewless map exposed a zero-sized preview surface and division by zero; missing packs/nonpositive dimensions now leave no image, and drawing guards empty rectangles. Both failures were investigated and retested. The plan's maximum-index table is also corrected: a 256×256 diamond reaches row/column 511 at different cells, with maximum allocated index 261,889, not 262,143.

Representative Debug loads were 317–339 ms for flat 125×125 and 1,067–1,116 ms for flat 256×256, with peak working sets around 291 MB and 340 MB respectively. The decode vector remained 5,784,965 bytes. Stock Grand Canyon is a separate workload. Three repeated stock timing pairs produced full/bounded pooled medians of 5.104/4.560 ms, with overlapping ranges; no fixed latency guarantee is claimed. The owning plan contains all samples and methodology.

### Findings the measurements produced

- fps-fix decision gate, provisional: ground vehicles move 1 to 4 px per tick
  in the golden sessions, aircraft up to 7, bullets 6, particles 8. Largest
  lepton delta seen is 57. Tick span measured 31 to 33 ms at `GameSpeed=2`
  (campaign) and 47 to 49 ms at `GameSpeed=3` (skirmish). The visible win is
  air, projectiles, and particles, as the plan predicted. The gate is not
  closed: the calibration cases and a heavy scene were not exercised.
- map-size baseline: `sizeof(CellClass)` is 164 bytes in both configurations;
  a 256x256 map costs about 20.5 MiB of cells plus the 1 MiB pointer table.
  Stock `G_CANYON` in Debug loads in 0.62 s, peaks at 284 MB working set, and
  `Encroach_Shadow` costs 3.9 to 5.2 ms per call. The IsoMapPack5 decode
  buffer is 5.8 MB of which 2.9 percent is used.
- `plans/map-size-changes.md` D4 is wrong about `DisplayClass::Encroach_Fog`:
  it already iterates the playfield through `Reset_Iterator` / `Iterate`
  (`code/display.cpp:2943-2954`), not the full index space. Only
  `Encroach_Shadow` (`code/display.cpp:2893`) does the full scan. The decode
  buffer sizing spans `code/display.cpp:3258-3264`.
- Replay focus behavior is documented in `docs/TESTING.md` on `map-size/phase-1`. The September 4 claim that skirmish was safe during desktop use was not supported: source verification shows the focus wait applies to both campaign and skirmish sessions, and a minimized launch can still lead to mouse capture. All game launches are deferred while the user needs the desktop.
- Resizing an existing map's `[Map] Size` header is not a valid 256x256 test:
  `In_Radar` moves the diamond, so authored cells and spawn waypoints fall
  outside the playfield. The 256x256 runtime measurement needs an authored
  map.
